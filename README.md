## Overview

Following a series of devastating cave-ins, you step into the Saffron Pyramid as its lead archaeologist.  Will you be the one to uncover its legendary Am-Ba Hart, or just another body left in the dark?

A 30-45 minute singleplayer adventure for vanilla Minecraft.
